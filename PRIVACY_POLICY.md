# Privacy Policy for dressMio

**Effective Date:** May 21, 2026

**Last Updated:** May 21, 2026

## 1. Introduction

dressMio ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our App.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly

**Clothing Photos and Images:**
- When you use dressMio to photograph or upload clothing items, we collect and process these images
- Photos are used for AI-powered clothing detection, categorization, and analysis
- We store processed metadata about your clothing items (color, type, material, pattern, etc.)
- Original photos may be stored temporarily for processing and permanently for your wardrobe database

**User Preferences and Style Information:**
- Outfit preferences and style choices you select
- Favorite clothing combinations and saved outfits
- Occasion tags and styling notes you add
- Color and style preferences you indicate

**Account Information (if applicable):**
- Email address (if you create an account)
- Username or display name
- Password (encrypted)

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically

**Device Information:**
- Device type, model, and operating system
- Device ID and unique identifiers
- App version and build number

**Usage Information:**
- Features you use and how frequently
- Time and duration of app usage
- Screens you visit and actions you take
- Search queries and filters you apply

**Camera and Photo Library Access:**
- We request permission to access your device's camera and photo library
- We only access photos you explicitly select or capture for the app
- We do not access other photos on your device without your permission

### 3.2 Service Improvement
- Improve and optimize the App's features and performance
- Analyze usage patterns to enhance user experience
- Fix bugs and technical issues
- Develop new features based on user needs

### 3.3 Communication
- Send you app updates and feature announcements

### 3.4 Analytics and Research
- Understand how users interact with the App

- Generate anonymized statistical reports

## 5. Data Security

### 5.1 Security Measures

We implement comprehensive security measures to protect your information, including:
- End-to-end encryption for sensitive data
- Secure HTTPS connections for all data transmission
- Encrypted storage of photos and personal information
- Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
- Secure authentication and access controls

### 5.2 Data Retention

- **Clothing Photos**: Stored as long as you maintain your account
- **Outfit Combinations**: Stored as long as you maintain your account
- **Usage Data**: Retained for up to 12 months for analytics purposes
- **Account Information**: Retained while your account is active

You can request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

### 5.3 Limitations

While we implement strong security measures, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your information.

- **Right to Non-Discrimination**: We will not discriminate against you for exercising your CCPA rights

To exercise these rights, contact us at: privacy@dressmio.com

## 9. European Privacy Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Union or United Kingdom, you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

- **Right to Rectification**: Correct inaccurate personal data

- **Right to Data Portability**: Receive your data in a portable format
- **Right to Object**: Object to certain processing activities

To exercise these rights, contact us at: privacy@dressmio.com
