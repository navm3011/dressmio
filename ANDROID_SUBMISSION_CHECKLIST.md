## Important Notes

### Google Play Store Requirements

1. **Privacy Policy:** Must be accessible and accurate
2. **Permissions:** Must be justified in app description
3. **Content Rating:** Must be completed before submission
4. **App Size:** Should be under 150MB (APK)
5. **Target API Level:** Must be current (API 34+)

### Common Rejection Reasons

1. **Misleading description** - Description doesn't match app features
2. **Broken functionality** - App crashes or features don't work
3. **Policy violations** - Violates Google Play policies
4. **Inappropriate content** - Screenshots or description contain inappropriate content
5. **Privacy policy issues** - Missing, inaccessible, or inaccurate privacy policy

### Timeline

- **APK generation:** 10-20 minutes
- **Developer account setup:** 24 hours (after $25 payment)
- **Content rating approval:** 24 hours
- **App review:** 1-3 days (typically 24 hours)
- **Total time to launch:** 3-5 days
