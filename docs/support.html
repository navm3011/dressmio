dressMio Support

dressMio is an AI-powered wardrobe organizer that helps you photograph clothing, organize wardrobe items, and create outfit suggestions.

Contact Support

For help with dressMio, email support@dressmio.com. Please include your device model, iOS version, and a description of the issue. Do not include passwords or private account information.

Common Questions

Camera or photo access: Open iOS Settings, select dressMio, and enable Camera or Photos access.

AI categorization: Use a well-lit photo with one clothing item clearly visible, then retry the analysis.

App problems: Force-quit and reopen dressMio. If the issue continues, contact support with the steps that reproduce it.

Privacy

Photos and wardrobe information are used to provide the app’s organization and outfit-planning features. For privacy questions, contact support@dressmio.com.
